About

This is a toolset to allow for collaborative planning and design for various creative projects! Just add water :P Coded in part by hand but largely by Claude Code. So, feel free to break into the code and fix anything that comes up, whether by hand or with an LLM. I was inspired to make this because I felt scatterbrained at times while organizing my projects. Obviously tools like Google Docs and Notion have their place. But, I just wanted to try something new! Cheers – Aisha
