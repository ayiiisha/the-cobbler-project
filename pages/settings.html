Storage

Projects are kept in this browser's database, which has room for far more than the old 5 MB limit. Uploaded files (images, audio, PDFs) are kept in the same database, separately from the projects.

Backups

Your projects live only in this browser. Clearing the browser's site data, reinstalling it or losing the computer loses them, so keep a copy somewhere else.
