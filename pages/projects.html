Sync folder

Work on projects together without uploading them anywhere: everyone picks the same shared folder (for example a shared Google Drive folder) and the projects you choose stay up to date through it. Your projects stay in your browser and in that folder only; the website never sees them.

Setting it up with Google Drive
  1. Install Google Drive for desktop (drive.google.com/drive/download) and sign in. Your Drive appears as a drive on your computer.
  2. One person makes a folder in Google Drive (e.g. “Cobbler sync”) and shares it with everyone (Share › Editor).
  3. Everyone else: in Google Drive on the web, open “Shared with me”, right-click the folder › Organize › Add shortcut › My Drive, so it appears on their computer.
  4. Here, click Choose sync folder and pick that folder (Google Drive › My Drive › Cobbler sync). Allow the app to edit files there.
  5. Tick Sync on the projects to share. Others see them under “Projects in the folder” and click Join.

Needs Chrome or Edge on a computer. Your browser asks again for permission now and then: click the sync note in the sidebar. Without it, use Share, Email and Import below; they work everywhere.

Differences settled automatically

Where you and a collaborator both changed the same thing, syncing kept the newer change. Check them here, and switch to the other version if it was the right one.
